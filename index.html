LumaBounce Privacy Policy

This page explains how LumaBounce handles information.

Effective date: August 11, 2026

1. Who we are

LumaBounce (package name com.ofsgames.lumabounce) is provided by OFS Games. This policy applies to the LumaBounce mobile game.

2. Information handled by the game

LumaBounce does not require an account. Game progress, stars, settings, language, sound and haptic preferences, Luma Coin balances, unlocked hints, and purchase entitlement cache may be stored locally on your device. OFS Games does not currently operate a server that receives this local gameplay data.

3. Advertising and consent

Depending on the app version and your region, LumaBounce may use Google AdMob to provide rewarded and interstitial advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, general location derived from IP, ad interactions, consent choices, and diagnostic information. Where required, a Google User Messaging Platform consent form is shown before advertising requests are made. Rewarded ads are optional.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

4. Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, payments are processed by Google Play. OFS Games does not receive your full payment-card details. The game may receive product identifiers, purchase status, and purchase tokens needed to verify and restore purchases. Google's handling of payment information is governed by its own policies.

5. Analytics

The current game architecture records limited gameplay events only in local debug builds and does not send them to a network analytics service. If network analytics is introduced, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before that data is collected.

6. Purposes and legal choices

Information is used to operate and save the game, remember settings, prevent fraud, verify purchases, serve and measure ads, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations. Where consent is required, you may accept, reject, or manage eligible advertising choices through the consent message and the app's privacy options when available. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier through your device settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app, subject to any device backup features you control. Advertising and purchase providers retain information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Because OFS Games does not currently maintain player accounts or a gameplay-data server, it ordinarily cannot identify or remotely delete local save data.

8. Children

LumaBounce is a general-audience game and is not specifically directed to children. Advertising and consent behavior will follow the age-audience settings selected for the app in Google Play and applicable law. If you believe a child's personal information has been handled improperly, contact us using the method below.

9. Security and international processing

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information handled by the game. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Third-party providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their safeguards and applicable law.

10. Your rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing, or withdraw consent. For privacy questions, use the privacy request form. Do not include passwords, payment details, advertising IDs, or other sensitive information in a public request.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the app, providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
